Our story

One oven.
Ten years of fire.

We started with a hand-built oven, a sack of flour and a promise to keep things simple: great ingredients, cooked with care, served by people who love what they do.

Our dough rests for 48 hours. Our tomatoes come from one family farm. Our wines come from people we’ve met.

— Marco & Lucia, owners
